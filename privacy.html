Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

AlansCinematics ("we," "us," "Alan") is a small photography business based in Phoenix, Arizona. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or inquire about a session, how we use it, and the choices you have. Plain language, no surprises.

AlansCinematics is a photography brand operated by Alan Pirnazarov, based in Phoenix, Arizona.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit the inquiry form on this site, you choose to share:

  • First name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Session type
  • Preferred date
  • Location (if you have one)
  • Any details you include in the message field

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect Social Security numbers, payment card data, or government identification through this form.

Automatic Information

Like most websites, basic technical information (such as your browser type, device type, and pages visited) may be logged by the hosting provider for security and performance purposes. This site does not currently run third-party advertising trackers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use what you share to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and discuss your session
  • Schedule and plan your shoot (dates, locations, details)
  • Deliver your final gallery
  • Follow up about your experience or share related session offerings, only when relevant

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.

3. How the Form Is Delivered

Inquiries submitted through this site are sent to Alan's business email via a third-party form delivery service (FormSubmit). That service processes the submission solely to forward it to us. Once received, your information is stored in the email inbox and any session-management tools Alan uses to keep track of clients.

4. Photos and Galleries

If you book a session, photos taken during your shoot are delivered through a private online gallery. Galleries are not public and are shared only with you via a private link. We may showcase select images from sessions in our portfolio, on Instagram (@alanscinematics), or in marketing materials. If you would prefer your images not be used publicly, just let Alan know — written requests are honored.

5. Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies, primarily for basic site functionality (such as fonts loaded from Google Fonts). It does not currently use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

6. Data Retention

Inquiry emails are retained as long as they are useful for ongoing communication or recordkeeping. Client galleries are typically available for a limited period after delivery (clients are notified before galleries expire). You can request deletion of your inquiry or contact details at any time.

7. Your Choices

You can:

  • Ask what information we have on file about you
  • Request corrections to that information
  • Ask us to delete your inquiry or contact details
  • Opt out of any follow-up communications

To make any of these requests, email alanscinematics@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Family session inquiries are submitted by parents or guardians.

9. Third-Party Links

This site links to Instagram and uses Google Fonts. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external sites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be noted clearly.
